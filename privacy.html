Overview

effingFocus ("we", "us", "our") is a productivity application designed for freelancers and independent professionals. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

By using effingFocus you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

What Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the app.

  • Account information — your name, email address, and profile photo provided by Google when you sign in with Google.
  • App data — tasks, client folders, time logs, call records, and expense entries you create inside the app.
  • Usage preferences — settings such as daily hour goals, weekly goals, and notification preferences.
  • Subscription status — whether your account is on a free trial or paid plan.

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any device sensors.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and sync the app across your devices
  • To authenticate your account securely via Google Sign-In
  • To calculate your productivity insights (time tracked, hourly rate, weekly progress)
  • To send transactional emails related to your account and trial
  • To process payments and manage your subscription

We never use your data for advertising. We never sell your data. We never share your data with third parties except those listed below who help us operate the service.

Third Party Services

We use the following trusted services to operate effingFocus:

Firebase (Google) — Authentication and database. Your data is stored securely in Google's cloud infrastructure. Firebase Privacy Policy

Stripe — Payment processing. We never see or store your card details. Stripe handles all payment data. Stripe Privacy Policy

Google Sign-In — Account authentication. We receive your name, email, and profile photo only. Google Privacy Policy

Vercel — App hosting and delivery. Vercel Privacy Policy

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase's secure cloud infrastructure. Firebase uses industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to your authenticated account only.

We do not store passwords. Authentication is handled entirely by Google Sign-In.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently deleted within 30 days. You can request deletion at any time by emailing hello@effingfocus.com.

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — request that inaccurate data be corrected
  • Deletion — request that your data be deleted
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to how your data is processed

To exercise any of these rights email us at hello@effingfocus.com. We will respond within 30 days.

GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, our legal basis for processing your data is your consent (provided when you create an account) and the performance of a contract (providing the service you signed up for). You have the right to withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

CCPA (California Users)

We do not sell personal information. California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of their data, and opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell it).

Children's Privacy

effingFocus is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Cookies

effingFocus does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Firebase may use session tokens stored in your browser to keep you logged in. These are essential for the app to function and cannot be opted out of while using the service.
